Data Deletion

App: 어부가 된 고양이 : 고양이 어부 타이쿤 (Google Play) · Developer: snailhouseNdevelop (달팽이집개발)

The Game has no sign-up and no servers of its own. Game progress is stored on your device. You can delete it yourself or request deletion as described below.

1. Delete progress stored on your device

  1. Open Settings → Apps on your device and select 어부가 된 고양이.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear data and confirm. (Menu names may differ slightly by device.)
  3. Progress such as money, unlocked facilities, research, cat coins, decorations and settings is deleted immediately.

Uninstalling the Game also deletes the progress on the device. A purchased first purchase pack is managed by your Google Play purchase history and is restored when you reinstall.

2. Delete cloud saves (if you signed in to Play Games)

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or Settings → Google → Play Games).
  2. Go to Delete account and data in its settings.
  3. Select 어부가 된 고양이 and delete the game data. The saved game is deleted.

3. Advertising ID

You can reset or delete the advertising ID used for rewarded ads (Google AdMob) in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

4. Request deletion by email

If you cannot delete your data with the methods above or have questions, email snailhousendevelop@gmail.com with the subject “어부가 된 고양이 data deletion request”. We will process it within 30 days and let you know the result.
