Privacy Policy

“어부가 된 고양이 : 고양이 어부 타이쿤” (the “Game”) respects your privacy and handles only the minimum information needed to run the Game. This policy explains what information is used in the Game and how.

Effective date: 2026-11-27

1. Information the Game collects directly

The Game does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location. There is no sign-up and the Game does not run its own servers. Game progress (money, unlocked facilities, research, cat coins, purchases, decorations, settings, etc.) is stored only on your device.

3. Ads and consent

The Game only shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch by tapping a “Watch an ad …” button; no ads appear automatically. In regions where consent is required (e.g. the European Economic Area), a consent form is shown on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time from “Ad privacy settings” in the Game’s settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Retention and deletion

Progress stored on your device is deleted when you clear the app’s data (Settings → Apps → 어부가 된 고양이 → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the Game. Cloud saves can be deleted from Google Play Games settings. Deletion of information processed by third-party services follows their policies. See Data Deletion for details.

6. Security

Information exchanged with third-party services is sent over encrypted connections (TLS).

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.
