Privacy Policy

“Facility Engineer” (the “Game”) respects your privacy and only handles the minimum information needed to run the Game. This policy explains what information is used in the Game and how.

Effective date: 2026-11-22

1. Information the Game collects directly

The Game does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and it does not run its own servers. Your game progress (stars, hint tickets, purchased items, settings, etc.) is stored only on your device.

2. Information processed by third-party services

These services handle information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Ads and consent

The Game only shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch by tapping a button. Where consent is required (for example, in the European Economic Area), a consent form is shown at first launch, and you can change your choice at any time from “Ad privacy settings” in the Game's settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Retention and deletion

Progress stored on your device can be deleted with “Delete all progress” in the Game's settings or by uninstalling the app. Cloud saves can be deleted in your Google Play Games settings. Deletion of information processed by third-party services follows their policies.

6. Security

Information exchanged with third-party services is sent over encrypted connections (TLS).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the new effective date on this page.
