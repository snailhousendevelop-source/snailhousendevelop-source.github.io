Data Deletion

App: Facility Engineer: Pipe Puzzle (Google Play) · Developer: 달팽이집개발 (snailhouseNdevelop)

This game has no sign-up and no game server of its own. Your progress is stored on your device. You can delete it yourself or ask us to help, as described below.

1. Delete progress stored on your device

  1. Open the game and tap Settings (the gear) in the office lobby.
  2. Tap "Delete All Progress" and confirm.
  3. Your progress (stars, hint tickets, Daily Check and Super Check records, etc.) is deleted right away.

Uninstalling the app also deletes the progress on your device. Purchased items (Remove Ads, outfits) are kept in your Google Play purchase history and are restored when you reinstall.

2. Delete cloud saves and leaderboard scores (if you signed in to Play Games)

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or your device's Settings → Google → Play Games).
  2. Go to the account and data deletion menu in its settings.
  3. Choose Facility Engineer and delete its game data. Saved games and leaderboard scores are deleted together.

3. Advertising ID

The advertising ID used for rewarded ads (Google AdMob) can be reset or deleted in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Request deletion by email

If you need help or have questions, email snailhousendevelop@gmail.com with the subject "Facility Engineer data deletion request". We will handle it within 30 days of receiving it and let you know the result.
